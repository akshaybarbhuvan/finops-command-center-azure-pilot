# FinOps Command Center — Leadership One-Pager

**Enterprise FinOps Governance & Optimization Platform** · local demo edition (illustrative data)

## Problem
Cloud spend is visible but not accountable. Optimization recommendations pile up in reports with no owner, no deadline and no proof of impact. Finance sees estimates that never reconcile to the bill; engineering sees findings without context; leadership cannot tell which decisions are theirs.

## Solution
A governance and accountability layer over cloud cost data that turns every optimization opportunity into an owned, tracked, verified outcome — with one source of truth for executives, FinOps and engineering.

## Why FinOps Command Center
- **Accountability by design** — every recommendation has an owner, an SLA, a ticket and an audit trail.
- **Potential vs realized, kept separate** — savings count only once verified against billing.
- **Role-specific experiences** — leadership decisions, FinOps portfolio control, engineering work queues, platform administration.
- **Evidence first** — each recommendation shows what, why, how much, risk, technical evidence and next action.
- **Enterprise-ready operating model** — swappable ticketing, role-based access, SSO-gated pilot/production modes.

## Key capabilities (demonstrated)
| Inform | Optimize | Operate |
|---|---|---|
| Spend trend, run-rate, MTD | Recommendation center & lifecycle board | Owners, SLA and escalation signals |
| Budgets, forecast, 80/90/100% thresholds | Compute, storage and commitment views | Ticketing linked to recommendations |
| Allocation & showback (BU, app, cost center, team) | Savings tracker: funnel & waterfall | Governance score & policy exceptions |
| Anomaly center with triage | Quick wins (low risk, high confidence) | Leadership reports (CSV / print) |

## Business value (qualitative)
- Shorter path from finding to realized savings through clear ownership and SLAs.
- Credible savings reporting for Finance — realized figures are verified, not projected.
- Leadership attention focused on decisions: unowned high-value items, pending approvals, budget risks.
- Better cost allocation and governance hygiene through visible tagging and ownership gaps.

*No ROI is claimed from demo data. A pilot measures realized savings against the organization’s own billing.*

## Demo highlights
- Executive Overview answers: what we spend, where it goes, what we can save, what is realized, who owns it, what is at risk, what needs a decision.
- Hero journey: a $1.08M/yr SAP HANA rightsizing opportunity goes from *unowned* to *savings verified* across Executive → Engineering → FinOps, and every dashboard updates.
- FinOps Copilot answers leadership questions from the same calculations and links to the records.

## Current scope
Local, offline demo with deterministic illustrative data; working workflows, role behavior, calculations and reports. No live Azure, client, ticketing or identity connections.

## Future roadmap (directional, not commitments)
- **Next:** persistent data store, live Azure Cost Management and Resource Graph ingestion, deeper commitment modeling, advanced analytics, enterprise ticketing and SSO integrations.
- **Future:** broader cloud coverage, policy-as-code guardrails, approval-gated automated remediation, natural-language analytics with cited answers.
